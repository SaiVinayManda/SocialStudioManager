## Decision to test

Prefer **C: a common interface with independently selectable engines**, initially qualifying MoneyPrinterTurbo for straightforward narrated stock videos and keeping OpenMontage disabled until its control, licensing and security gates pass. Preserve the independent FFmpeg long-form-to-reels route. Do not chain both engines through every job or promise automatic fallback between visually different workflows.

MoneyPrinterTurbo has the more concrete headless submission/result path and a simpler root licence. OpenMontage has stronger source-level graphics and editing building blocks, but its production orchestrator is an agent following instructions, and its broader executable surface increases qualification effort. Neither has earned a quality, speed, reliability or hardware score here. Final selection depends on the four controlled scenarios in [VIDEO_ENGINE_BENCHMARKS.md](VIDEO_ENGINE_BENCHMARKS.md).

- **I — inference/proposal:** an engineering assessment or integration design, not an upstream guarantee. **U** means not established in the reviewed scope; it does not prove universal absence.

## Local/free versus paid dependencies

OpenMontage's coding-agent reasoning has a separate account/usage cost even when media providers are local. Its core requirements do not fully install optional transcription, CLIP, Piper, voices, browsers or rendering runtimes. CLIP uses `from_pretrained`; HyperFrames can fetch npm packages at first use. Auto-setup and even some doctor/probe paths can download/execute packages. They were not run. [CLIP loader](https://github.com/SaiVinayManda/OpenMontage/blob/195eab24478dc9363326aec437aefc3701635ddf/lib/clip_embedder.py), [runtime tool](https://github.com/SaiVinayManda/OpenMontage/blob/195eab24478dc9363326aec437aefc3701635ddf/tools/video/hyperframes_compose.py).

MoneyPrinterTurbo can use approved external or local LLM endpoints, prepared scripts, local media/audio and several stock/TTS providers. The local Ollama branch exists, but no local service/model has been provisioned. Whisper may fetch a model when selected; the reviewed comments say Edge failures do not silently select it. No paid API, account session, free-key provider or external TTS service has been activated. Its root licence does not establish entitlement to any provider or subscription. [LLM routing](https://github.com/harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo/blob/fd5127e81721f36f0a85aee21b298cc5f4452f42/app/services/llm.py), [configuration](https://github.com/harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo/blob/fd5127e81721f36f0a85aee21b298cc5f4452f42/config.example.toml).

Official Google documentation now describes Antigravity CLI headless output and API-key/ADC authentication, and Gemini CLI documents headless authentication. This establishes a documented candidate route, **not a validated local fallback** or an entitlement from a Gemini Pro subscription. Keep fallback disabled until a pinned Windows CLI, owner-provisioned scoped authentication, unattended restart/expiry behavior, exact model access, limits and cost are verified. No authentication or inference call was made. [Antigravity authentication](https://antigravity.google/docs/install/), [headless mode](https://antigravity.google/docs/cli/headless/), [Gemini CLI authentication](https://geminicli.com/docs/get-started/authentication/).

## Licence and commercial review

OpenMontage's inspected root is **AGPL version 3**. Commercial use is not inherently forbidden. Distribution/conveyance can require source and notices; section 13 imposes a source-offer obligation for users interacting remotely with a modified network-enabled version. Whether a particular Studio wrapper, modification, hosted/internal access pattern or combined distribution is covered requires review of that concrete design. A separate process/API is an architectural boundary, not a blanket exemption from copyleft. Do not assume every generated video becomes AGPL solely because the tool rendered it; copied assets or covered material require separate analysis. The exact `-only` versus `-or-later` grant must be established from applicable notices, not the generic licence text alone. [Pinned licence](https://github.com/SaiVinayManda/OpenMontage/blob/195eab24478dc9363326aec437aefc3701635ddf/LICENSE).

MoneyPrinterTurbo's inspected root is **MIT**: retain the copyright/permission notice for covered copies/substantial portions. Its dependencies, FFmpeg build configuration, fonts, bundled songs, models, voices and stock content have independent terms. Neither root licence clears music, trademarks, likenesses, stock downloads or provider-generated assets for every commercial channel. [Pinned MIT licence](https://github.com/harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo/blob/fd5127e81721f36f0a85aee21b298cc5f4452f42/LICENSE).

Remotion is an additional commercial gate for OpenMontage, not an MIT dependency assumption. Its official terms describe free eligibility for individuals/companies up to three people and paid terms for others, including automation products. Verify eligibility, applicable version and any compatibility/distribution obligations before selecting that renderer; do not buy or activate a licence implicitly. Piper implementations and voice model cards, optional HyperFrames/GSAP assets, and all stock/music must also enter the bill of materials. [Remotion terms and eligibility](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/pricing).

## Maintenance and security findings

Both clones contain recent October 28 commits and tests. These are evidence of activity, not executed CI results or stability guarantees. OpenMontage has source/event/checkpoint improvements but a wide skill/tool surface; MoneyPrinterTurbo has recent progress/TTS/auth changes and Windows smoke configuration. Full dependency advisories, all optional providers, issue-response latency and long-term maintenance remain unassessed.

The requested OpenMontage fork's browser/issue pages could not be retrieved. Its files refer to the calesthio project; the following **upstream issue reports are contextual and were not independently reproduced against this fork**:

- [#864](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage/issues/864) reports a Manim static-scan bypass. The inspected `math_animate.py` does accept and execute caller-supplied Python through Manim; a denylist is not an OS sandbox. Exclude this tool from the first proof of concept. This is a reported vulnerability, not an exploit executed here.
- [#855](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage/issues/855) reports black video tails with slowed cinematic clips. Include slowed-footage/frame-tail checks if that path is used.
- [#626](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage/issues/626) reports impersonating binary installers. Retain the exact requested repository identity; do not fetch similarly named installer repositories.

MoneyPrinterTurbo [#1711](https://github.com/harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo/issues/1711) reports Gemini TTS empty audio, while the inspected HEAD improves its diagnostics; that is not proof of an upstream service fix. [#1342](https://github.com/harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo/issues/1342) reports stuck tasks/limited termination. These support explicit provider-failure, timeout and cancellation tests rather than a reliability rating.

Source-confirmed risks requiring controls:

| Risk | Required response |
| --- | --- |
| Agent instructions, generated React/HTML/Python, dynamic imports and package/runtime fetches | Treat all as code from an untrusted source. Use a separate least-privilege process/runtime, no owner home or secrets, fixed reviewed tool registry and denied undeclared egress. Importing modules is execution, not read-only inspection. |
| OpenMontage loads `.env` at import, dynamically discovers tools, and can detach Backlot | No owner environment inheritance or `.env` copy. Disable unneeded tooling/board startup; use scoped brokers and verify full child-tree termination. Its cost/events/checkpoints remain engine evidence, not the authoritative approval ledger. |
| MoneyPrinterTurbo defaults include broad binding, optional blank API key and multi-task concurrency | Prefer isolated per-job CLI initially; otherwise private binding, approved auth, concurrency one and strict wrapper. No public listener. |
| MoneyPrinterTurbo generation can cross-post | Force `upload_post_enabled=false` and `upload_post_auto_upload=false`; no publishing keys; egress deny. Test that generation cannot invoke publishers. OpenMontage publish stages are also excluded. |
| Cross-brand assets, paths, prompt injection, corrupted output and retries | Immutable channel/brand versions; per-channel access checks; asset IDs/hashes, strict paths/export; independent decode checks; idempotent local/provider reconciliation. No unreviewed automatic retry or provider switch. |

## Strategy comparison and next decision

| Strategy | Strength | Limitation | Phase A position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — OpenMontage primary | Best source-level fit for charts, custom branding, varied editing and clip-factory planning | Requires a production control layer; broad generated-code surface; AGPL and renderer terms; Windows end-to-end unproven | Qualify as an optional creative engine. Do not select as sole unattended engine now. |
| B — MoneyPrinterTurbo primary | Concrete topic-to-video CLI/API; narrower integration; MIT root | Graphics and semantic highlights need external work; cancellation/durable recovery still need wrapper guarantees | First engine to qualify for stock/narration scenarios. Do not calculate an overall production score before output evidence exists. Proposed final weights are quality 30%, unattended reliability 25%, maintainability 20%, commercial viability 15%, engineering effort 10%; resource ceilings and legal acceptance are mandatory gates regardless of score. Costs and hours are estimates until observed.
